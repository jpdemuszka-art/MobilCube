# Google Ads Editor import

Generated by `python3 ads/build_import.py` from the keyword research (`ads/keywords/*.research.json`), the autocomplete-verified additions of 2026-09-30, and the ad copy in the script. Re-run it after editing copy, prices, budgets or landing URLs. It refuses copy that breaks Google's character limits, and it refuses negatives that would block an enabled keyword.

Launch plan behind these files: `docs/08-launch-analysis-2026-09-30.md` (5,000 $/month, Manual CPC).

## How to import

1. Google Ads Editor → open the account → **Account › Import › From file…** (or paste from a spreadsheet).
2. Pick the CSV. In the mapping dialog, confirm each column matches the Editor field with the same name (`Criterion Type` values are `Exact`, `Phrase`, `Negative Exact`, `Negative Phrase`).
3. Review the proposed changes, fix any red rows, **Finish and review changes**, then **Post**.
4. In the web UI afterwards:
   - Attach `Negatives - FR` to the FR campaigns and to Marque and Concurrents. Attach `Negatives - EN` to the EN campaigns and to Marque and Concurrents.
   - Set locations, radius tiers and borough exclusions (docs/08 §8), the ad schedule and conversion goals.
   - Add the call asset with a forwarding number, plus the location, price and image assets. Then un-pause.

## Before you post

- Landing URLs default to pages that exist on mobilcube.com today (`LP_MODE=site`). Once the vehicle and terrace pages from `landing/` are published, re-run with `LP_MODE=lp LP_BASE=https://your-host python3 ads/build_import.py`.
- Prices in ads (350 $, 270 $, 289,75 $, 180 $, 300 $ delivery) must still match the site on launch day.
- Ads say "Soumission en 60 secondes" / "Quote in 60 seconds" because the site's button leads to a quote form with a callback, not an instant checkout.
- Paused on purpose: boats and jet-skis (marinas are cheaper; large boats do not fit), generic city storage searches in Montreal, Laval and the West Island (ten chains bid there), and "self storage near me" terms. Enable them only as experiments.
- Search volumes behind the bids are estimates until the Keyword Planner pass (docs/08 §11).
