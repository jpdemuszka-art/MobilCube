# 01 · Competitive analysis — Montreal storage market, fall 2026

Research date: 2026-09-26. Every number below was read on the company's own site or on the cited page that day. Where a competitor hides a number, the entry says **not published**. Two competitors (Cubeit, PODS) and one incumbent (Montreal Mini-Storage) got full deep-dives; the rest got lighter coverage because the research session hit its rate limit — those are marked *light*.

## 1. Direct mobile-storage competitors

### Cubeit (StorageVault) — the best-funded direct rival
Sources: cubeit.ca/quebec/montreal, cubeit.ca/fr, cubeit.ca/deals, rental agreement PDF, GTM container GTM-5LNBCKT.

- **Offer:** one 8×8×16 ft container ("128 feet of space"), not heated, 4-week prepaid cycles, minimum four weeks, rent **not published** (quote by postal code). Third-party comparison confirms rent + delivery + pickup + optional yard fee. Yard is in **Vaudreuil-Dorion** (far west, off-island).
- **Promo:** "Free local delivery" on all markets **until 2026-09-30**, within 25 km of a Cubeit location. Costco, Perkopolis, military and 100 $ referral programs.
- **Vehicles:** rental agreement forbids "vehicles, motorcycles, engines" and flammables; 4,750 lb load, 5,000 $ value cap. **They cannot serve the winter-vehicle market.**
- **Commercial:** generic retail/construction pages; no restaurant or terrace page.
- **Marketing machine:** 4 Google Ads conversion IDs, Google call tracking, Bing UET, Meta, LinkedIn, GA4, Google Optimize on the estimator, six tracking phone numbers. They **are** bidding and optimising on calls.
- **Trust:** 4.8/5 on 5,029 Google reviews site-wide; 4.7/5 on ~597 for the Vaudreuil location.
- **Weaknesses to exploit:** no price anywhere; one size; not heated; yard 45+ km from the East end and South Shore; no local 514/450 number, no direct tel: link (modal + callback); French pages carry English testimonials and typos ("rebais"); English-only quote flow and contract; promo lapses Sept 30 exactly when winter demand starts.

### PODS Canada — the brand searchers know
Sources: pods.ca (EN/FR Montreal page, /promo, /winter-car-storage, /business), GTM-KSFZLJL.

- **Offer:** 8, 12, 16 ft containers, month-to-month, first 30 days included, rent **not published** (quote drawer needs email). Third-party estimate for Montreal: C$240–370/month plus C$100–200 delivery (moving-calculator.net, March 2026, directional only). Laval storage centre (1975 rue John-Molson). Extended delivery charges beyond ~32 km of the centre.
- **Promo:** site-wide countdown code LAST30 / DERNIERE30 (up to 30 % off first month and delivery fees, expiring 2026-09-29); 3/6/12-month bundles with free delivery and 15–25 % off; EN promo page says 25 %, FR says 30 % (inconsistent).
- **Vehicles:** a real **Winter Car Storage** program in 8 Canadian cities including Montreal — **drop-off only at the Laval centre, cars only** (FAQ bans motorcycles, ATVs, watercraft, anything with fuel), stored in a heated facility, dedicated line (855) 715-3361, no price shown.
- **Commercial:** restaurant/hotel/retail pages in English that already talk about patio heaters and outdoor furniture.
- **Marketing machine:** two Google Ads conversion IDs, website-call conversion tracking, remarketing, Floodlight, Bing, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn; per-page tracking numbers. National TV/digital budget.
- **Trust:** 4.7/5 on ~800 reviews (Laval).
- **Weaknesses to exploit:** zero Montreal price transparency; winter car program requires you to drive to Laval and excludes motos/ATVs/boats; South Shore addresses pay extended delivery or are refused (redirect to U-Pack); 150 $ cancellation and non-refundable first month; contents protection quoted in USD; French footer still shows 2025 dates.

### GoCube — the local "cube" brand (*light*)
Sources: gocube.com/en, gocube.com/en/storage-montreal.

- Quebec company, 514 738-6843, Montreal + South Shore + North Shore, heated 24/7 warehouse.
- Small cubes (about 5 × 8 × 7 ft, 2,000 lb each): a 4½ apartment needs ~3 cubes. "From 95 $/mo" is for 3 cubes × 5 months, **transport excluded**. Pricing page returns 404; promo banner still says "30 % off … deliveries before March 31, 2026" (stale).
- **Weaknesses:** cannot take a car, boat or ATV; many small cubes for one household; stale promos; no vehicle or terrace pages.

### BigSteelBox (*light*, benchmark only)
Sources: bigsteelbox.com/locations, /storage/rent-buy, /resources/moving-new-province, /business/storage/restaurants.

- **Does not serve Quebec outside Gatineau**; no French site (fr/ = 404). Irrelevant as a service competitor, but Montreal users still search the brand and it publishes the clearest price benchmark in Canada: **20 ft container ~149 $/mo average + 39 $/mo yard storage**, delivery varies. Its restaurant page describes exactly the terrace use case (fall drop-off, winter at yard, spring redelivery) — copy the narrative in French.

### U-Haul U-Box, Entrepôt Mobile (DDO), Spaceful, Valet Dépôt (*not covered*)
The deep-dive agents for these were cut off by the rate limit. From the initial scan: U-Haul self-storage in Montreal starts at 99.95 $/mo with a "1-year price lock"; U-Box containers are 8×5×7.5 ft and exclude vehicles; Spaceful and Valet Dépôt are per-box valet services (LaSalle, seasonal messaging) that cannot take vehicles or a full terrace. Treat them as bid competitors on "entreposage Montréal", not as vehicle/terrace competitors.

## 2. Traditional self-storage (they bid on the same generic keywords)

### Montreal Mini-Storage — the promo machine
Sources: montrealministorage.com (EN/FR), terms page, parking directory.

- 24 facilities (island, Laval, La Prairie, Sainte-Julie, Varennes, Beauharnois, Laurentides). 4.7/5 on 3,108 Google reviews. Call centre 8 am–9 pm, live chat, SMS.
- **Promo:** "Save 50 % on up to 6 months rent" site-wide; move-in specials. **Hidden stack:** 29 $ setup + 18 $/month admin + taxes, and the price doubles after the promo; reviewers report annual increases.
- **Vehicles:** outdoor uncovered parking at ~15 sites: promo 42–146 $/mo (regular 84–292 $/mo), e.g. Laval Vimont 10×20 at 228 $ regular / 114 $ promo. No winter page, no boat/RV pricing page.
- **Commercial:** business page targets retailers, contractors, agents, medical; **never mentions restaurants, terraces, hotels or landscapers**. French business page 404s.
- **Gaps for us:** no facility in Longueuil, Brossard, Boucherville, Saint-Hubert; customer hauls everything; large drive-up units are expensive on the South Shore (8×20 La Prairie 510 $/mo regular).

### Depotium, Public Storage, SmartStop, StorageMart, Access Storage (*light*)
From the initial scan: Depotium runs a "Montreal car storage" page (no prices visible, 1-888-440-5050) and lists "entreposage mobile"; SmartStop advertises "up to 25 % online" and "1 month free" in Dorval, Laval, Notre-Dame West; StorageMart downtown pushes heated units for business; FindStorageFast reports Montreal 10×10 units averaging 236 $/mo (from 82 $). These chains will appear on "entreposage Montréal" and "mini entrepôt" auctions with bigger budgets: **do not fight them on generic head terms at launch; own the mobile, vehicle and terrace long tail.**

**Reading:** nobody in Montreal delivers a lockable steel unit to your driveway for your car, motorcycle, ATV or snowmobile. That is MobilCube's open lane, with two caveats: a 20 ft unit takes a car, a bike, two ATVs or a snowmobile trailer, but **not a 20 ft boat on a trailer** (the trailer makes it 23–24 ft) — target PWC, jet-skis and small boats up to about 16 ft on trailer; and warehouse storage of anything with fuel needs an explicit policy (drain to ¼ tank, disconnect battery) before we advertise it.

## 4. Commercial terrace / seasonal storage market (*light*)

- Caméléon Vert (furniture maker) stores customers' outdoor furniture November to mid-April with "limited space, book early". JML and other patio retailers do not store. Movers (Storage Olympique, Gareau) do full-service warehousing at moving rates.
- BigSteelBox and PODS have restaurant pages, in English, and neither delivers a heated option on the South Shore. Cubeit, GoCube and Montreal Mini-Storage have nothing.
- Demand trigger: Montreal boroughs require café-terrasses on the public domain to be dismantled at the end of the season, typically between mid-October and mid-November depending on the borough. **Exact 2026 dates per borough could not be fetched in this session — confirm with 3 restaurant clients or the borough permit pages and put the dates in the ads.**

## 5. What competitors do best (copy it) and do wrong (attack it)

| They do best | Apply to MobilCube |
|---|---|
| Cubeit/PODS: 4-step estimator, "no credit card", emailed estimate | Keep the 60-second price flow; add "sans carte de crédit" to the booking CTA |
| Cubeit/PODS: full call tracking + conversion tags | Install AW tag, website-call swap and call assets before spending a dollar (see `tracking/`) |
| PODS: per-segment phone numbers and pages (winter car, restaurant, hotel) | One landing page per segment with its own tracking number |
| Montreal Mini-Storage: simple headline offer, review badge everywhere | Publish a simple seasonal offer and show the Google rating on every page |
| BigSteelBox: restaurant narrative (drop-off, winter, spring redelivery) | Use it in French on the terrace page |

| They do wrong | Our counter in ad copy |
|---|---|
| No price anywhere (Cubeit, PODS, GoCube) | "Prix affichés. 270 $/mois sur 6 mois. Livraison 300 $." |
| Vehicles banned or drop-off-only | "On livre le conteneur, votre auto dort dedans. Moto, VTT, motoneige acceptés." |
| Yards far away (Vaudreuil, Laval) | "Entrepôt chauffé à Boucherville, 15 min de Longueuil et Brossard." |
| 16 ft or 5×8 ft units | "Un seul MobilCube de 20 pi = 3 cubes GoCube." |
| Promos that expire Sept 29–30 | Launch our seasonal offer Oct 1 and keep it live to Dec 1 |
| Hidden fees (29 $ + 18 $/mo, 4-week prepaid cycles) | "Aucun frais d'administration mensuel. Dépôt remboursé." |
| English-first or English-only | French-first everything; English campaign for the West Island |
