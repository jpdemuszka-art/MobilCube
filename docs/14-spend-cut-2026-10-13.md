# Spend cut, October 13, 2026

Applied live in account 194-768-4780 on 2026-10-13, at the owner's request ("lower investments: few conversions so far and the CPC is quite high"). The repo files match the account again: `ads/build_import.py` (budgets, the `CUT_*` blocks), the Editor CSVs and `ads/api-specs/`.

## Why

| Oct 7–12 (6 days) | Value |
|---|---|
| Spend | 746.53 $ |
| Clicks | 257 |
| Average cost per click | 2.90 $ |
| Bookings (booking form conversions) | 2 |
| Clicks that became a booking | 0.8 % |
| Cost per booking | about 373 $ |
| Spend on clearly irrelevant searches | about 113 $ (15 %) |

The break-even CPCs in docs/13 §4c assume that 4 % of clicks become a lead. At the 0.8 % seen so far, every break-even CPC is about 5 times lower: for example about 0.81 $ for a mobile unit instead of 4.05 $. No bid level is profitable at that rate, so the plan is to spend less until the booking form (docs/13 F11) and the lead count (WPForms vs Google Ads, docs/10 step 5) are fixed, and to stop paying above break-even.

## 5. What to expect

These are estimates, not measured results.
- **Spend:** at most about 96 $ a day and about 2,920 $ a month, down from 149 $ and 4,530 $.
- **Cost per click:** no click above 2.80 $ in the FR mobile and Terrasse ad groups listed in §2, 3.50 $ in EN Mobile storage and 3.00 $ in EN Winter vehicle and EN Commercial patio. The average should drop most in EN Mobile storage (4.59 $ so far) and EN Winter vehicle (3.75 $).
- **Trade-off:** a lower bid also lowers ad rank, so these ads will show less often and sometimes in a lower position. FR and EN mobile storage were losing about two-thirds of their impressions to budget, so part of that loss is absorbed by budget that now goes further.
- **Waste:** the negatives and the 2 removed keywords block the RV, boat, U-Haul, warehouse-leasing and shipping-container searches seen in the search terms since Oct 7. On Oct 12 alone, RV and U-Haul searches cost 18.39 $ (10 % of the day).
