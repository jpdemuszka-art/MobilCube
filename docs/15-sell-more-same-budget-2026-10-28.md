# Strategy review, October 28, 2026: how to sell more without spending more

Owner's question: "analyse my strategy and see how I can sell more without spending more." Ad budget fixed at 96 $/day. This review rests on the repo's own history (docs/13 audit, docs/14 spend cut) and on the live site as fetched on October 28. **There is no performance data after October 14** (the Supermetrics trial expired October 15), so every expected effect below is an estimate, flagged as such. Five independent analyses (conversion funnel, budget and bids, waste and negatives, ads and offer, free channels) were run and then checked line by line against the repo and the live pages; only what survived that check is here.

## 1. The verdict in five lines

1. **The ads are not the problem. The page after the click is.** Oct 7–14: 911 $, 332 clicks, 2 bookings (0.6 % of clicks, about 456 $ per booking). The plan assumed 4 %. At 4 % the same 911 $ would have bought about 13 leads and about 4 bookings. Cutting bids from 2.00 $ to 0.50 $ and then 0.75 $ did not change that ratio; it only removed the traffic.
2. **The 0.6 % may itself be wrong.** The booking and call tags were never tested end to end, and the cookie banner refuses ad tracking by default (Law 25), so visitors who refuse cookies and then book are invisible. The first task is to count the real leads since October 7 (WPForms emails and phone calls) and compare with the 2 that Google Ads shows.
3. **The site changed its prices on October 20–21 and the ads did not follow.** The pricing page and both booking forms now say Liberté 240 $/month and Avantage 200 $/month (6 months+), while every ad still says 350 $ / 270 $ / from 180 $, the home page still says 350 $, and the EN booking form still shows the whole old grid. The owner must pick one price list today; then the ads must match it (Quebec consumer law and Google policy both require the ad price to match the page). A 240 $ headline also gets more clicks than a 350 $ one for the same money.
4. **At 0.75 $ the budget is not the constraint, the bids are.** Keyword Planner puts about 1.2–1.4 % of the account's searches within reach at 0.75 $, 20–22 % at 1.50 $, 44–50 % at 2.00 $. The few clicks the account can still buy at 0.75 $ go to the lowest-value keywords ([storing motorcycle], "patio furniture storage"). Re-splitting budgets changes nothing until bids are tiered. The way to sell more inside 96 $/day is to put the bids where the evidence is (vehicle season, brand, competitor-brand, South Shore) and park the rest.
5. **The cheapest bookings of the next five weeks are outside paid search:** answering every lead within the hour, Google Business Profile and reviews, free Kijiji / Marketplace vehicle listings, re-contacting everyone who already asked for a price, and one referral partner. The car season ends with the December 1 winter-tire deadline; everything below is sequenced against it.

## 7. Expected effect, honestly

- If the real lead rate is 0.6 %: tiered bids inside 96 $/day buy roughly 6–9 bookings a month at 150–300 $ each instead of 456 $, mostly cars and motos. Not a business.
- If the form and callback fixes lift the rate to 2 % (half the planned 4 %, and the 2025 storage benchmark is 4.65 %): the same clicks give about 3× the bookings, and the break-even CPC for a mobile unit rises from 0.61 $ to about 2.00 $, which is what makes the 1.50–2.00 $ tiers sustainable without touching the budget.
- Everything in §5 is additive and does not depend on the ads at all. In a 5-week car season, the hour-by-hour callback and the listings are the most likely sources of the next bookings.

The one number that decides the rest is the real lead count since October 7 (§2 item 1). Do that first.
