Frequently asked questions

What fits in 160 sq ft?

The contents of a 5 ½ apartment: living room, full bedroom, kitchen, appliances, boxes. Or 20,000 lb of equipment. 7′8″ × 7′5″ door for large furniture.

Do I need a permit for my driveway?

On private property, usually not. On the street, your borough requires a temporary occupancy permit. Call us, we know the South Shore and Montreal rules.

How much room for delivery?

About 75 ft in a straight line for the truck and 14 ft of overhead clearance. Stable, level ground. If placement is impossible on the day, a $250 fee applies: check our list 24 h ahead.

What can't be stored?

In the warehouse: flammables (fuel, solvents, cylinders), toxic products, food, hazardous waste.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Move from Freedom to Advantage at any time in your customer portal to lock a lower rate.
