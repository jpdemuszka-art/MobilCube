How it goes

Designed for a Monday-morning patio closing.

Drop-off at your business

Class 8 truck, placement in your parking lot, back yard or the street if you hold an occupancy permit. Needs 75 ft in a straight line and 14 ft of height.

Your team loads

7′8″ wide door, dolly-level floor. Chairs stacked, tables on their sides, umbrellas flat, heaters upright and strapped. Locks and straps available.

On site or with us

Keep the container on site all winter (24/7 access) or we take it to our heated Boucherville warehouse. In April we bring it back the day before you open.

Frequently asked questions

Can the container stay on the street?

On your lot or parking, yes, all winter. On the street you need a temporary public-domain occupancy permit from your borough; the simplest is to load the same day and let us take it to the warehouse.

Can I retrieve something mid-winter?

On site: 24/7 access. In the warehouse: by appointment within 2–3 business days.

Are propane patio heaters accepted?

Yes, without the cylinder. Propane cylinders and anything flammable are prohibited in the warehouse.

How does invoicing work?

Invoice in the company's name, payment by card or bank transfer, automatic debit available. A $200 deposit is refunded after inspection on return.

And in spring?

You pick the return date in your customer portal; changes are free up to 72 h ahead. The container arrives the day before and your team rebuilds the terrace in the morning.
