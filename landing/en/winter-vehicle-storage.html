How it works

Three steps, no double handling, no appointment at a facility.

We deliver the container

Class 8 truck, precise placement in your driveway (75 ft straight clearance, 14 ft height). Delivery $300, 15 km from Boucherville included, then $2/km.

You drive the vehicle in

Ground-level ramp, 7′5″ door height. Disconnect the battery, inflate the tires, put the cover on: done.

In spring, we pick it up

The container stays at your home all winter with 24/7 access. When you no longer need it, we collect it ($300, same rule).

Frequently asked questions

Is the container heated in my driveway?

No. A container at your home is a dry, locked shelter out of the snow and salt, like an unheated garage. For heated warehouse storage, call us: vehicles with a fuel tank are subject to conditions.

Can I leave fuel in the tank?

At home, yes, as in a garage; add a stabilizer. Our Boucherville warehouse prohibits flammables: ask us for the applicable rule before booking the warehouse option.

How much room do you need?

About 75 ft in a straight line for the truck and 14 ft of overhead clearance (wires, branches). Stable, level ground. If placement is impossible on the day, a $250 fee applies, so check with us in advance.

Can I change my dates?

Free more than 72 h ahead; $50 between 48 and 71 h; $150 between 24 and 47 h; $250 same day.

Is it insured?

The container is locked and you keep the key. Your belongings stay covered by your home or auto policy: ask your insurer, most cover a vehicle stored on your property.
